FAQ

Questions couples ask us

What does wedding flower decoration cost in Jaipur?

Transparent packages: ₹15,000+ for haldi/mehndi functions, ₹35,000+ for full wedding mandap setups, and ₹75,000+ for palace & destination weddings. Your exact quote depends on venue, flowers and season — send your date on WhatsApp and you'll have an exact number the same day.

Have you decorated at Jai Mahal Palace, Fairmont or other palace venues?

Yes — our florals have been part of real weddings at Jai Mahal Palace and Fairmont Jaipur, credited by professional planners like The Wedding Art and The Stagecraft. You can verify it yourself on our Instagram.

We live outside India — can we book you remotely?

Absolutely — most of our destination clients plan everything over WhatsApp and video calls: flower palette options, mood references, then a written all-inclusive quote. The quote you approve is the price you pay. No surprises for NRI couples.

How far in advance should we book?

Jaipur's peak season (Nov–Feb) palace dates fill 6–9 months ahead, so earlier is better. For local functions like haldi or mehndi, 1–2 weeks is often fine — always ask, we'll tell you honestly if your date is possible.

How many weddings do you take per date?

A limited number — so your setup is never rushed and we're on-site personally for every booking. This is exactly why dates fill early in season.

Do you travel outside Jaipur?

Yes — destination weddings across Rajasthan: Udaipur, Jodhpur, Pushkar, Ajmer, Neemrana and more. Travel and stay are quoted transparently upfront.

Which flowers do you use?

Fresh, seasonal flowers only — marigold, rose, orchid, jasmine and more. Premium imported flowers are available on request for palace themes.

Is setup and teardown included?

Yes — concept, flowers, materials, transport, full on-site setup and teardown of our structures are all in the quote. Nothing extra appears later.

Do you work with our wedding planner?

Very happily — most of our palace-hotel work comes through professional planners. See the planners' section below.

How do we confirm the booking?

A token advance blocks your date; the balance is settled on the event day. Simple written confirmation on WhatsApp — no paperwork headaches.
