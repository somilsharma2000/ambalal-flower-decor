## PART B — OWNER BAN KE SOCHO: BUSINESS KAISE GROW KAREGA

*Agar main Ambalal Flower Decor ka owner hota, to 90 din me ye karta:*

### Month 1 — FOUNDATION (leads ko track karna seekho)
- **Har ek enquiry database me daalo** — naam, date, budget, status (New → Contacted → Quoted → Won/Lost). Aaj se pata chalega: kitne leads aaye, kitne bane, kahan se aaye.
- **Rule: 15-minute reply.** Wedding leads girti hai sirf slow reply se. Din me 3 baar WhatsApp check.
- **Google reviews = sabse bada weapon.** Har shaadi ke baad dulhan ke papa/mummy se 2-minute review maango. 10 reviews ke baad "wedding decorator Jaipur" me Maps me top me dikhna start hoga. Reviews free hai aur har lead ko convert karta hai.
- **Google listing ki photos har mahine update** — Google "active business" ko upar dhakelta hai.

### Month 2 — PARTNERSHIPS (Jaipur ka asli lead source)
Jaipur me decorators ke clients nahi, **channels** milte hain:
- **Wedding photographers** — wo har shaadi me couple se baat karte hain. 5-10 photographers se bolo: har referral par ₹2,000-5,000 commission ya free car decor unke shoots par. Ek photographer = saal me 10-15 shaadiyan.
- **Tent houses & venues** — Kukas/Fairmt-style venues, banquet halls: "aapke client ko special rate, humko referral."
- **Makeup artists & mehndi artists** — same wedding ecosystem, same clientele.
- **Priests/pandits & shaadi bureaus** — old-school but Jaipur me abhi bhi sabse zyada referrals yahin se aate hain.
Sabko WhatsApp catalog bhejo. Catalog = aaj ka brochure.

### Month 3 — SEASON ENGINE (Nov-Feb Jaipur ki peak season)
- **Instagram reels 3x/week** (kit ka 30-day plan follow karo) — reels hi destination weddings ke clients laati hain (₹75k+ wali Royal package yahin se bikti hai)
- **"November-February dates filling" urgency** — limited dates asli scarcity hai, use karo
- **Old clients ko re-engage karo:** "Aapki shaadi ko 1 saal — anniversary par fresh flowers 20% off" — repeat + referral dono
- **Baarat car decor ko loss-leader banao** (₹2,500) — chhota kaam, naya customer, poora shaadi usko milega aage

### Owner ke numbers (dimaag me rakhna)
- Aaj avg booking ~₹25k ho to: sirf **har mahine 2 extra bookings** = +₹50,000/month revenue
- Iske liye chahiye: ~10-15 extra enquiries → 3 reviews + 2 photographer partners + website funnel ye de hi dega
- Aapki fees (₹12k + ₹2k/month) usko 1 extra booking me 3x recover — **ye hi pitch line hai: "Sir, 1 nayi shaadi me poora saal ki fees nikal aayi."**
